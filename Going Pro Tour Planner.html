Getting into draws without a ranking

How acceptance works for an unranked player at M15 and M25 level, and what to do for each event on your plan.

Who gets in, in order

  1. 1. ATP singles ranking
  2. 2. ITF World Tennis Ranking
  3. 3. World Tennis Number (WTN), if verified at 70%+ confidence
  4. 4. National ranking (top 500)
  5. 5. Unranked

The WTN used is the one held on the Monday before the entry deadline. A verified WTN is your best lever: it puts you ahead of every unranked entrant.

Wild cards

  • Awarded by the tournament organiser with approval of the host national association (USTA in the US).
  • Email the tournament director 3–4 weeks out. Include your Notre Dame record (21-7, nationally ranked No. 50), career-high ranking, recent results and WTN.
  • Ask for a qualifying wild card as well as main draw. Qualifying WCs are far easier to get.
  • Enter through Tour Zone anyway, so you are on the list if the WC doesn't come.

Qualifying draw size matters

  • US events: 32-player qualifying. Without a WC or a strong WTN you'll usually sit on the alternates list.
  • Monastir and Sharm El Sheikh M15s: 64-player qualifying, every week. This is where unranked players realistically get matches.
  • Stacking 3–4 weeks in one place cuts travel cost and lets you sign in on site every week.

Deadlines and sign-in

  • Entry: Thursday 14:00 GMT, 18 days before the event's Monday.
  • Withdrawal: Tuesday 14:00 GMT, 13 days before. Withdraw after that and you risk a fine.
  • Qualifying sign-in: 18:00 local time the day before qualifying. On-site alternates sign in with the ITF Supervisor in person, or email then phone. Email alone doesn't count.
  • Entry fee: up to US$40 per event.

Before your first entry

  • Buy an IPIN membership and log in to World Tennis Tour Zone. That's where you enter and withdraw.
  • Register or verify your WTN at worldtennisnumber.com.
  • Complete the ITIA Tennis Integrity Protection Programme if Tour Zone asks for it.

Status steps used on this page

  • Considering: on your radar.
  • WC requested: email sent to the TD.
  • Entered: entry made in Tour Zone.
  • Accepted Q / MD: you're on the acceptance list.
  • Alternate: plan to sign in on site.
  • Withdrawn or Skipped: off the plan, kept for your record.
